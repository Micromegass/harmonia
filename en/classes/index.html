The classes

Group or private, kids or adults, from your first basic step to advanced level. Here's how Harmonia Baila works:

Formats

Group classes
The group's energy does half the work. Grouped by level and age, with limited spots so the teacher actually sees you.
Private lessons
One-on-one or as a couple, at your pace and towards your goals: a wedding dance, polishing technique, or progressing twice as fast. Great if you prefer learning in English.
Kids & adults
Playful children's classes and adult classes at every level. It's never too late (or too early) to start.

Levels

  1. Beginner

    From zero, no judgment. Rhythm, basic step and confidence.

  2. Intermediate

    Figures, turns and partner connection. This is where the flavor starts.

  3. Advanced

    Musicality, personal style and stage-level technique.

The rhythms

Salsa

The rhythm that runs in our veins

From the basic step to the turns that own the dance floor. Cali-style, línea and salón salsa. Technique, flavor and street soul.

Bachata

Slow, close and full of feeling

Dominican and sensual bachata: partner connection, body waves and musicality. The rhythm people fall for fastest.

Urban styles

Pure energy to let loose

Reggaeton, dancehall and urban choreography. Cardio, attitude and flow, perfect if you want your workout to feel like a party.

Tropical rhythms

Cumbia, merengue and Caribbean heritage

The rhythms of Colombia: cumbia, merengue, porro. Skirts, handkerchiefs and traditions danced with pride.

Kids' dance

Play, move and grow with music

Classes where dance is a game: rhythm, coordination and confidence for life.

Contemporary dance

Expression, technique and freedom

For anyone who wants to explore movement beyond the dance floor: contemporary technique, improvisation and expression.

What about prices?

Rates depend on the format and how often you come. Message us and we'll put together a plan that fits. No strings attached.
